About Chris Mene

Chris Mene is passionate about helping people discover purpose, deepen their faith, and become intentional about the lives they are building.

Through teaching, speaking, writing, and meaningful conversations, Chris creates spaces where people can think deeply, ask honest questions, and grow into the people they were created to become.

His work sits at the intersection of faith, personal growth, leadership, and everyday life. Rather than separating spiritual growth from the realities of life, Chris believes that faith should shape how we think, how we relate with people, how we lead, and how we navigate our purpose.

“Purpose is not simply about discovering what you were made to do. It is about becoming the person who can faithfully live it out.”
